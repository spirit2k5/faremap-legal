Permanent action

Delete a FareMap Account

Use this page to request deletion even when FareMap is not installed.

ACCOUNT DELETIONDeleting an account is permanent and cannot be undone.
Inside the app

Delete inside FareMap

  1. Open FareMap and sign in.
  2. Open My Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm permanent deletion.
Without the app

Request deletion by email

Email FareMap support from the address connected to your account. Include your FareMap account email and clearly request permanent deletion.

Email deletion request

Data

What is deleted

FareMap will delete or anonymise the authentication account and associated FareMap data, including profile information, role, votes, confirmations, favourites, budgets, waiting requests, live-driver records and other account-linked records. Limited records may be retained only when necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling or legal obligations.

Driver Pro subscription

Cancel billing separately

Deleting a FareMap account does not automatically cancel an active Driver Pro subscription purchased through Google Play. To stop future billing, cancel the subscription separately in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions before or after requesting account deletion.

Process

What to expect

  • FareMap may verify that you own the account.
  • Deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days.
  • You do not need to reinstall FareMap to request deletion.
  • After deletion, deleted account data cannot be restored.
